Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Emmax Concepts ("we") handles information in connection with our website and our internal tool, the Emmax Outreach Engine (the "App").

1. What the App is

The App is an internal business tool used only by Emmax Concepts. It is not offered to the public. It connects to our own Google account to send our business emails, to detect replies, bounces and unsubscribe requests, and to keep our prospect list in our own Google Sheet.

2. Google user data the App accesses

  • Gmail (read-only and compose): to create and send our outreach emails and follow-ups from our own mailbox, and to read replies and delivery-failure notices to those emails so that we stop contacting anyone who replies, bounces or unsubscribes.
  • Google Sheets: to read and update our own spreadsheet of business prospects, suppression list, activity log and settings.

The App only accesses the Google account of Emmax Concepts. It does not access any other person's Google account.

3. How the data is used and stored

Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the functions above. It is processed on our own computer and stored in our own Google account and local files. We do not sell it, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Business contact information

We contact businesses using business contact details that the businesses publish themselves (for example on their own website) or that appear in openly licensed business listings such as OpenStreetMap. We may use an email verification service to check that an address can receive email before writing to it. We keep a record of who we contacted and when, and a suppression list of anyone who asked not to be contacted.

5. Your choices

If you received an email from us, reply "Unsubscribe" (or write to the address below) and we will add you to our suppression list and not contact you again. You can also ask us to delete the information we hold about you.

6. Security and retention

Access credentials are stored only on our own computer and are never published. We keep outreach records only as long as needed to run our business and honour unsubscribe requests.
